# Hi, I'm Dimitris Georgantzis Garcia 👋 

*Ph.D. Management | MSci Mathematics & Physics | MSc Data Science (ongoing)*

I combine quantitative modelling, experimental methodologies, and data science to understand human behaviour—especially in the context of sustainability and the circular economy. My work spans research design, data collection, and analysis, with a growing focus on reproducible workflows and applied AI. Outside research, I produce music, run live shows, and co-manage an independent record label.
